Because of the ShareAlike term, these derived images stay under CC BY-SA 3.0.

J and Z: `j-motion.mp4` and `z-motion.mp4` (and their stills `j.jpg`, `z.jpg`) were recorded by
the ASL Awareness project team and are not from the source above. `i.webp` and `d.webp` plus a
drawn path (see `signLibrary.json`) are only used as a fallback if a clip fails to load.
